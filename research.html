Navigating the Optimization Gap: Challenges and Improvements in Deep Learning Model Deployment

A survey of 93 papers on why deep learning models that excel on offline benchmarks often underdeliver under real latency, cost, and reliability targets, a mismatch the paper calls the optimization gap. It breaks the gap into five dimensions (performance, efficiency, compatibility, observability, and operational robustness), maps mitigations at the model, compiler, system, and pipeline levels, and identifies five research directions that need more attention, including cross-hardware portability and treating deployment-time security and compliance as first-class concerns.

Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR), Vol. 87, Article 26 · 28 pages · Authors: Lovina Dmello, Blaise Dmello, and Linnet Tuscano · DOI 10.1613/jair.1.22113

CascadeAuth: Separating the Resource Knob from the Decision Boundary in Risk-Adaptive Authorization Cascades

A study of fast-slow cascades for real-time fraud screening, where a compact first tier settles confident cases and an uncertainty band forwards the hard ones to a richer second tier. On the public ULB credit-card benchmark, escalating the most-uncertain 20% of transactions matches the second tier's fraud recall at 71.6% lower compute, while sliding the band center to steer load fails in three ways. The paper concludes that the resource actuator in a cost-aware cascade must be a separate variable from the decision boundary, and gives a pre-deployment check that catches each failure offline.

2026 IEEE 8th International Conference on Cognitive Machine Intelligence (CogMI) · pp. 310 · Author: Lovina Dmello · DOI 10.1109/CogMI71712.2026.00042 · CogMI 2026 accepted papers
