US PATENT · FILED 2026 · PATENT PENDING

Attestation-Gated Assembly and Training of Multi-Party Fraud Graphs Inside a Confidential GPU Boundary Using Keyed Linkage Tokens and Policy-Filtered Output Release

A method for securely assembling and training multi-party fraud-detection graphs entirely within a confidential GPU boundary. Parties are joined through keyed linkage tokens with hardware attestation gating access, and results are shared only through policy-filtered output release, so sensitive data never leaves the trusted boundary.

U.S. Provisional Utility Patent Application under 35 U.S.C. 111(b) · Application No. 64/121,881 · Inventor: Lovina Dmello.
