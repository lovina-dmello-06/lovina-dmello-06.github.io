How I review

What authors and chairs can expect from my reports.

Structured reports

Each review gives a summary of the contribution, its strengths and weaknesses, questions for the authors, and minor points, so the reasoning behind the recommendation is easy to follow.

Claims against evidence

I check whether the experiments support the claims: baselines, ablations, statistical reporting, reproducibility, and how accurately related work is described.

Constructive feedback

I point out what works as well as what does not, and frame concerns as specific questions and changes the authors can act on.
